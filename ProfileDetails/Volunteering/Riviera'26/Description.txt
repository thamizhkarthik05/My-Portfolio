As a member of the Stalls Committee for Riviera, the mega cultural fest at VIT University, I was responsible for reaching out to vendors and coordinating with them to set up stalls on campus. This role helped me overcome my initial hesitation in approaching new people and improved my communication and networking skills.

Although I faced several rejections while contacting vendors, the experience taught me persistence and resilience. Eventually, I was assigned as the Point of Contact (POC) for one of the participating shops, where I learned the importance of building professional relationships and maintaining good coordination with external partners.

In addition, I worked closely with faculty members as the only student representative involved in distributing food coupons for the entire university community participating in the festival. This opportunity allowed me to collaborate with experienced professionals, understand organizational responsibilities, and adapt to working with people from different age groups and backgrounds.

Throughout the event, the faculty members provided valuable guidance and constructive suggestions that helped me improve my work. At the end of the festival, I was appreciated by the faculty members for my contribution and dedication. Overall, this experience was unexpected but extremely rewarding, helping me grow personally and professionally.
